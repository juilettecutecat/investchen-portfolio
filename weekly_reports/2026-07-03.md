## Self-Learning

**What worked:**
- **CORE no-stop structure validated under maximum pressure:** MU -15.8% this week, yet MU is STILL +25.84% from IC entry ($790). Any EXPLORE-style stop below entry would have been hit weeks ago during MU's pre-earnings volatility, converting a +25.84% unrealized gain into a loss. The CORE dual-investor structure is doing exactly what it was designed to do.
- **BN stop governed correctly:** Stop at $42.78 executed as planned. -£14.02 realized, cash freed for SNDK/TSM redeployment. No emotion, no override.
- **NTRA stop captured profit despite gap:** The gap-through was painful (slippage -£37 vs expected), but the stop STILL generated +£24.85 realized gain on a £300 position. Without the stop, NTRA from $271.45 (Jun 30) to $216.50 (Jul 1 open) would have been a -20% gap loss on a £391 position = -£78 unrealized.

**What didn't:**
- **BE catalyst-day gap-up rule violation:** This is a process failure, not an analysis failure. The Brookfield $25B thesis is structurally correct. IC added at $326.60 open (gap-up +12.8%) rather than waiting 30-60 minutes. The stock reversed -17% to close at $270.89. New rule: "On >5% gap-up catalyst days, wait 30-60 min for price discovery." Estimated cost of violation: -£19.66 unrealized on that specific add.
- **NTRA overnight gap risk:** NTRA stop at $242.73 assumed normal market conditions. No pre-market circuit-breaker. For EXPLORE positions in small/mid-cap biotech/genomics, overnight gap risk is larger than in large-caps. Consider: (a) smaller EXPLORE sizing for binary-catalyst stocks, (b) keep EXPLORE max £150 for single-investor stocks in volatile sectors.
- **Sector concentration cost:** IC holds MU + TSM + SNDK (AI memory/foundry) + BE (AI power) = 4 of 6 CORE positions in the same macro theme. When the AI-semiconductor narrative rotated to supply glut fears, IC bore full brunt. AMZN and UBER as diversifiers covered only 20% of portfolio. No fix in current framework (CORE rules govern), but noted for long-run portfolio construction.
- **SNDK timing:** SNDK entry Jun 29 at $2,090 was technically correct (dual-investor trigger met). However, the SK Hynix Nasdaq ADR listing (Jul 10) was a known risk that materialized faster than expected. Future dual-investor CORE entries should explicitly note near-term competitive catalysts as modifiers to position timing even when triggers are met.

**Rule updates effective Jul 7, 2026:**
1. **CATALYST-DAY GAP-UP TIMING (active from Jul 2, reaffirmed):** On >5% gap-up catalyst days, wait 30-60 min for price discovery before adding to CORE positions. Applies to all tickers. [Violation: BE Jul 2]
